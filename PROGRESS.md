Two known quirks, taste for U1: a runner standing still on a tilted plank slides slowly down it (about 0.16 tiles/s: the rules set the runner's speed along the ground every step and Matter's contact pushes along the slope), and the plank cannot flip as fast as a runner runs across it (they cross in about 0.9 s and it turns at about 0.4 to 1 rad/s, so it is mostly still tipped to the entry side when they leave).

61. What a finish is (src/logic/race.js, course.js). The line is the left edge of tile `finish.col`: the runner's centre reaching it (x at least col, in tiles) ends the race, in the same branch as the flags (a runner below the course or waiting to be put back cannot finish, and flags passed in the same step come first). The race is then `finished`: `t` stands still and IS the finish time, `stepRace` does nothing more, `controlsLive` is false, and the scene gives NO_INTENT so the runner coasts to a stop in about half a tile (the e2e asserts under 1.5). The time is the step the line was crossed in (1/60 s), not interpolated to the exact line. A course with a `finish` needs a `minTime` (the fastest anyone could possibly run it, for N3's sanity check on results) and `minTime` needs a finish; the finish must be right of the spawn and of the last flag, and ground must run from the line to the wall, so nobody falls away after finishing. The smoke check also holds every `minTime` at or under distance / diveSpeed (test-race: 5 against 5.77), so a real run can never be rejected as too fast.
